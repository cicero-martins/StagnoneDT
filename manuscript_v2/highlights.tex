\textbf{Relative effects of seagrass canopy, wave coupling and bed mobility on Lagrangian transport in a shallow lagoon}

Cicero Martins Jr., Mauro De Marchis, Giuseppe Ciraolo, Carmelo Nasello, Antonino Maltese

\vspace{0.6cm}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=0.5em]
  \item Seagrass canopy drag raises drifter skill by 0.12, five times the effect of waves
  \item Only the gauge inside the vegetated basin separates the canopy treatments
  \item An uncalibrated mobile bed mimics the canopy through implausible bed erosion
  \item A uniform initial column inflates the canopy effect from 0.12 to 0.19
  \item Canopy parameters come from published measurement, none fitted to observations
\end{itemize}
