\textbf{Transport validation of a wave-coupled lagoon model: interacting
effects of seagrass roughness and bed mobility}

Cicero Martins Jr., Giuseppe Ciraolo, Mauro De Marchis

\vspace{0.6cm}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=0.5em]
  \item Six model configurations agree on water level but differ by 30\% in drifter skill
  \item Seagrass roughness and bed mobility change transport only when applied together
  \item A mobile bed corrects how far particles travel, roughness corrects where
  \item Only the combined configuration reproduces the observed drifter transport rate
  \item Surface current exceeds bed current by 1.9 to 4.5 times at sub-metre depth
\end{itemize}
