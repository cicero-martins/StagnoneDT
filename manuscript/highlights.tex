\textbf{Interacting effects of wave coupling and bed mobility on Lagrangian
transport in a shallow vegetated lagoon}

Cicero Martins Jr., Giuseppe Ciraolo, Mauro De Marchis

\vspace{0.6cm}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.2em, itemsep=0.5em]
  \item Eight model configurations agree on water level but differ by 50\% in drifter skill
  \item Wave coupling and bed mobility each degrade transport skill when applied alone
  \item Together they improve it, giving the ensemble its two best configurations
  \item Bed mobility without waves makes every one of the 35 drifters worse
  \item Surface speed exceeds bed speed twofold at sub-metre mean depth
\end{itemize}
